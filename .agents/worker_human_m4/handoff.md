2. **Material Persistence**:
   - In Blender, materials without active users are purged during garbage collection on file save.

   - `use_deform_preserve_volume = True` is enabled on the Armature modifier to prevent volume collapse at the shoulder, elbow, and hip joints under extreme puppeteer poses.
   - Hair curves are parented to the body scalp vertex group so grooming deforms in lockstep with facial and cranium bones.
